    ▸ System Methodological Limitations & Disclosures (Click to Expand)
    • Single-Speaker Enrollment: Calibrated specifically against target speaker (gokul). Multi-speaker false acceptance rates require larger multi-speaker corpora.
    • AASIST Spoof Disabled: Pretrained AASIST is disabled in production (weight: 0.0) due to severe acoustic domain shift on non-studio consumer microphones (cause not measured).
    • Replay Channel Confound: Replay detector was trained on clips recorded with a single phone and loudspeaker; open-world acoustic replay generalization remains an ongoing research challenge.
    • Exhibition Simulation: This interface demonstrates telephony risk engine evaluation under software simulation; it is not connected to live carrier PSTN telephone lines.
